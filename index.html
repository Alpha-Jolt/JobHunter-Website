How JobHunter works — 6 deliberate steps

  1. Tell us what you want. Complete a short questionnaire with your target roles, salary, locations, and skills.
  2. We scrape jobs for you. Our rule-based engine pulls fresh listings from Naukri, Indeed, and more into your private dashboard.
  3. Browse and select roles. Review your personalized job feed and pick the specific roles you want to target.
  4. AI tailors your resume. The AI reads each job description and reorders your existing experience to highlight the most relevant parts — it never fabricates credentials.
  5. You approve, we send. Every tailored resume and cover letter sits in an approval queue. Nothing is sent without your explicit sign-off. We then deliver via your Gmail or Outlook.
  6. Track and improve. Monitor response rates, application history, and email threads from a single dashboard.

Our core principles

  • No fabrication. The AI curates and reorders your existing resume content. It never invents experience, skills, or credentials that aren't already on your resume.
  • You approve everything. No resume variant or cover letter is sent without your explicit sign-off. Nothing moves forward without you.
  • Deterministic by default. The scraper and mail sender are rule-based systems. AI is confined to the one step it belongs in — resume tailoring.
  • Your data stays yours. The scraped job dataset has no external export endpoints. It is private by default, not just by policy.
  • Compliance by design. Scam detection, abuse prevention, placement verification, and GDPR/DPDPA workflows are built into the roadmap, not afterthoughts.
  • Placement guarantee. Phase 3 skill programs come with a full refund if you are not placed. Mentor scores are tied to real outcomes.

Features — built in phases

Phase 0 — Core Engine (Completed, June 2026)

  • Multi-source scraper: A rule-based crawler pulls listings from Naukri and Indeed today, with LinkedIn and more coming in Phase 1. Deduplication and normalization are built in.
  • AI resume tailoring: Reads each job description against your resume and reorders your real experience to fit — highlighting what matters, inventing nothing.
  • Automated mail sender: Sends approved applications using static, human-written templates. No generative AI touches the send layer.
  • Approval queue: Every draft waits for your sign-off. Nothing is sent without it — you stay in control at every step.

Phase 1 — Platform Foundation (In Progress, July 2026)

  • Application tracker: Full thread history, response rates, and outcomes. An integrated mailbox pulls job-related email from your connected account.
  • Stealth mode: Automatically skips jobs at companies you have flagged and hides your activity from your current employer.

Phase 2 — Intelligence Layer (Planned, Q4 2026)

  • Scam detection: Confidence scoring flags suspicious listings before you see them. Free-webmail HR contacts are treated as low-trust by default.

Phase 3 — Skill Development & Monetisation (Planned, 2027)

  • Skill developer: A paid placement program run by verified professionals. Not placed? Your fee is refunded. Mentor scores are tied to real outcomes.
  • Mentorship program: 1-on-1 mentorship with industry experts to guide your career path.
  • Subscriptions & payments: Flexible plans for job seekers. Mentor commission and refund logic is automated.

Frequently asked questions

What is JobHunter?
JobHunter is an AI-powered, human-in-the-loop job search companion. We automate job discovery, tailor your resume to each role without fabricating experience, and prepare applications for your explicit approval.
Does JobHunter automatically apply to jobs?
No, and this is by design. JobHunter prepares your application, but every resume variant and cover letter sits in an approval queue. Nothing is sent without your explicit sign-off.
Does JobHunter customize my resume for every job?
Yes. The AI analyzes each job description and curates your existing resume content to highlight the most relevant skills and experience. It never fabricates or invents credentials.
How is JobHunter different from LinkedIn or Indeed?
LinkedIn and Indeed are job boards where you manually search and apply. JobHunter is a job search companion that sits on top of those boards, automating the discovery, tailoring, and drafting process for you.
Can JobHunter apply using my Gmail or Outlook account?
Yes. Once you approve an application, JobHunter uses a secure bridge to send the email directly from your connected Gmail or Outlook account, so you own the communication thread.
Is JobHunter free to use?
The core job search and application automation is free during beta. Advanced Skill Development Programs (Phase 3) will be paid, with a full refund guarantee if you don't get placed.
Is my data safe?
Absolutely. The scraped job dataset has no external export endpoints. We are building in public with privacy first, incorporating GDPR and India's DPDPA compliance into our roadmap.
Is JobHunter available outside India?
While our platform is currently optimized for the Indian job market (covering Naukri and Indeed India), it is not restricted. Job seekers globally can use JobHunter, and we plan to expand coverage to more international job boards soon.
Who is JobHunter built for?
JobHunter is designed for college students, recent graduates, unemployed professionals, and freelancers who need to scale their job search without losing quality or burning out.
Can I become a mentor?
Yes. We're onboarding mentors for Phase 3. Head to the For Who page and fill in the mentor form to join our verified professional network.
